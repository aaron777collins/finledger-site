Before you install

Finledger keeps your money data on your device. There is no account to create, so the ledger works as soon as you open it: import a CSV or add a transaction. The AI assistant is optional and needs a one-time model download inside the app, about 2.6 GB on phones and 3.7 GB on computers.

Every build here is the same code on every platform. Release notes live on the releases page. If something goes wrong, support tells you how to report it.
